# Work marketing workflows

## Create a campaign

1. Confirm actual Work execution and private inspection/delivery capabilities. In ordinary Chat, explain that creative production requires Work; no image fallback or generated artwork is permitted.
2. Read the packaged messaging, content, asset and visual references. Run `scripts/brand_preflight.py --surface work` against the installed skill. The versioned bundle is the production brand snapshot; no repository checkout or live GitHub fetch is required.
3. Define the audience, moment and action. Choose one message supported by the available evidence, qualifying Philippine route/account/commercial availability.
4. Draft clear British English. Record each factual claim's exact wording, source/date/scope, owner and approval status. Unsupported claims stay on Hold.
5. Follow `visual-generation-protocol.md` and `work-runtime.md`: choose a registered template/photo/CTA, render privately, inspect the actual result and run delivery validation.
6. Correct failed copy/fit privately and rerender in a new directory. Never edit brand sources or expose failed drafts.
7. Deliver only after a fresh `PASSED` receipt with `image_ready_for_delivery: true`. Include exact PNG, channel-ready copy, alt text, dimensions and the concise check outcome. An explicit copy-only request may omit artwork in Work.

## Review checklist

- Brand: approved unchanged logo, horizontal divider and Philippines descriptor, Noto Sans, registered palette, layout and photo/crop.
- Content/evidence: audience relevance, exact wording, British English, one CTA, current source/date/scope, approval status and no unsupported product/pricing/performance/compliance/endorsement claims.
- Accessibility: readable type, actual background contrast, no overflow or text collisions, meaningful alt text and no information conveyed by colour alone.
- Local fit: Philippine context without stereotypes; illustrative people are not employee/customer/testimonial proof.
- Destination: only `https://burstsms.com.ph/`, inserted by the renderer. No customer-facing Kudosity branding or external/invented links.
- Provenance: the shipped bundle passes integrity checks and the file is the exact deterministic output. No image generator, alternate logo, unregistered photo, recolouring or validation bypass.

Every failed or unavailable check blocks delivery. Check results are local evidence, not signed owner approval or proof that claims are true. The publication owner must still approve review-required wording and publication.

## Channel guidance and claim status

Landing pages, email, organic/paid social, SMS examples and sales material must retain the preserved messaging library's scope/evidence rules. Social requests in Work include checked artwork plus channel-ready copy by default. SMS examples use fictional/anonymised data, verified sender type, length/encoding and required opt-out language.

Use `Proposed copy`, `Verified capability`, `Approved for publication` and `Hold`. A verified feature is not automatically approved marketing wording. Preserve source/date/version, date checked, market/route/account scope, owner, approver/date, review date and superseded wording. Time-sensitive offers in archived approved examples must be reconfirmed before a new campaign flight.
