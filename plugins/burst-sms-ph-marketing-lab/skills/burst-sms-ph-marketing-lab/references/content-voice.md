# Content voice

## Voice

Write as a capable guide. Be direct, educational, and quietly personable. Give experienced readers something useful without sounding academic or sales-heavy.

## Rules

- Use British English and the Oxford comma.
- Address the reader as `you`; use `we` when speaking for the brand.
- Prefer active voice, short paragraphs, and concrete verbs.
- Explain abbreviations on first use.
- Link descriptive words, never `click here`.
- Avoid superiority claims such as `best`, `leading`, `number one`, or `premium` without evidence.
- Avoid vague urgency, hype, repeated exclamation marks, and all caps.
- Do not call something obvious.
- Do not repeat the same opening word across consecutive sentences or paragraphs when a natural alternative exists.

## Philippines localisation

Use English by default. Use Filipino or regional language only when the audience, channel, and reviewer support it. Localise examples through relevant industries, currency, time zones, compliance context, and customer needs rather than stereotypes.

## Calls to action

Use a specific next step, such as `Talk to our team`, `Explore SMS solutions`, `Review compliance guidance`, or `Start a trial`. Avoid generic actions such as `Learn more` when a clearer destination exists.

## Evidence

Label unsupported numbers and claims as placeholders. Current claims must link to an approved product, legal, compliance, or analytics source before publication.


Choose messaging for the customer and objective, rather than repeating compliance-led copy. Lead with a concrete relevant benefit and a substantiated reason to choose Burst SMS. For compliance/onboarding, use the dated owner-confirmed Philippines team expertise within its recorded scope; never promise guaranteed compliance or regulatory approval.
