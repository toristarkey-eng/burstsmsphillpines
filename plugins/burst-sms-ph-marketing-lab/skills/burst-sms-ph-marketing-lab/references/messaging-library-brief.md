# Burst SMS Philippines messaging library brief

This is the searchable plugin companion to the approved Word master in `../../../assets/approved/burst-sms-ph-messaging-library-brief.docx`. Current explicit Tori instructions take precedence for branding, call-to-action destination, approval status, and shared-platform context.

## Assignment and audience

Build a complete reusable messaging library for the Philippines commercial and marketing team, led by Grace Briones, Country Manager, Philippines. Support everyday content, enterprise outreach, partner conversations, sales enablement, and usable finished assets.

Lead with regulated and operational audiences: government and public services, financial services, healthcare, and utilities. Also cover retail and ecommerce, booking and service businesses, software platforms, agencies, and white-label or reseller partners.

## Mandatory customer-facing rules

- Promote Burst SMS only. Do not use Kudosity branding, logos, website links, or `powered by Kudosity` language.
- Use `https://burstsms.com.ph/` as the customer-facing CTA destination and campaign-tracking domain. Do not invent paths.
- Localise audience, examples, terminology, currency, timing, and requirements for the Philippines.
- Use clear English and British or Australian spelling by default. Use Filipino or regional language only for a defined audience with local review.
- Use PHP or `₱` only for approved Philippine pricing and Asia/Manila `UTC+8` for local schedules.
- Use fictional or anonymised sample data. Treat URLs, opt-outs, Sender IDs, encoding, and message length as delivery requirements.
- Shared Kudosity-platform capability is internal context, not a public brand story. Qualify every capability for Philippine market, route, carrier, account, release, and commercial availability.

## Message foundation

- Core line: `Business messaging for Philippine organisations.`
- Supporting line: `Connect customer communication to the way your business works.`
- Campaign platform: `Simple to start. Built to scale.`

These foundations remain proposed until specifically approved for publication. Lead with useful messages at the right moment, messaging inside existing workflows, recognisable sender identity, operational scale, and local practical guidance.

## Capability coverage

Inventory and qualify the complete shared platform before writing. Cover SMS campaigns; scheduling, personalisation, contacts, and segmentation; journeys and automation; APIs, SDKs, webhooks, and email-to-SMS; Branded Sender IDs; virtual numbers, two-way SMS, and Inbox; reporting and trackable links; white label, reseller, and multi-account management; WhatsApp Business API and RCS; MMS, short codes, HLR, and lookup; integrations, AI workflows, and managed services; and separately confirmed voice or SIP opportunities.

Do not transfer Australian or New Zealand availability to the Philippines. The source brief records that MMS is not supported for the Philippines. Alpha Sender IDs are not reply numbers. Treat RCS, WhatsApp, voice, numbers, routes, lead times, fees, receipts, throughput, and integrations as scope-dependent until verified.

## Evidence and approval

Every factual record needs the exact wording, source title and link, source date or version, date checked, market/route/account scope, owner, approval status, approver/date, review date, and superseded wording.

Use four statuses: `Proposed copy`, `Verified capability`, `Approved for publication`, and `Hold`. A verified capability is not automatically approved marketing copy. Do not publish unsupported delivery, performance, compliance, security, residency, government credential, customer proof, partner proof, pricing, or competitor claims.

Known unresolved source conflicts remain on `Hold`, including conflicting SMS length or encoding notes, registration and permit work, planned procurement tasks, future volume projections, and any historical quote whose exact approved wording is unavailable.

## Required library modules

- Brand and core positioning: brand rules, hierarchy, short and long descriptions, pillars, value proposition, approved terms, and exclusions.
- Industry and buyer messages: eight industry modules and variations for commercial, operations, technical, and procurement buyers.
- Product message cards: problem, benefit, mechanism, usable copy, example, limits, proof, and approval status for each capability.
- Sales enablement: pitch, discovery questions, objections, outreach, follow-ups, demo invitation, proposal opening, reactivation, and expansion copy.
- Website and campaigns: homepage hierarchy, industry-page structures, landing-page template, headline bank, subject lines, and CTA bank.
- Social and creative: Facebook and LinkedIn content covering use cases, Sender ID, white label, integrations, trust, and the local team. Include a finished supporting image unless copy-only is explicitly requested.
- SMS examples: at least two per priority use case, with sender type, character or segment check, required opt-out, and verified link handling.
- Governance: claims register, proof bank, source index, approved and rejected creative register, changelog, owner, and review schedule.

## Acceptance gate

All customer-facing assets must promote Burst SMS, use the Philippines website destination, and pass copy, image, metadata, alt-text, QR-code, and UI checks. No roadmap item may be presented as live. Do not use unsupported PH MMS, reply-to-alpha, AU-only compliance, RCS, or voice claims. Avoid em dashes, generic SaaS filler, forced slang, fabricated testimonials, and unsubstantiated superiority claims.

Every social post must include its supporting image, correct supplied lockup, readable layout, and appropriate alt text. Preserve exact approved artwork and quotes. Review the library monthly and whenever product, routing, pricing, carrier rules, or brand instructions change.
