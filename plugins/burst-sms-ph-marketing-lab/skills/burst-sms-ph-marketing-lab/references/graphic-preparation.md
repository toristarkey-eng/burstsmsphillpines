# Preparing supplied graphics

Use the reviewed `*-clean.png` variants in the catalogue for campaign rendering. Originals remain alongside them for source comparison. Tori approved the supplied illustrations, explanatory YOUR BRAND label, Burst-palette recolouring and cleanup on 10 October 2026.

## Accepted preparation method

Use mask-only preparation to preserve original RGB, lettering and objects. Remove detached tiny alpha components, apply a small morphological opening and one-pixel boundary erosion, and restore a narrow antialias transition capped by the original alpha. This is an asset preparation step, not runtime near-white removal. Never classify a subject by RGB brightness. Inspect narrow handles, rods, wheels, icon details and bubble tails. Do not use this illustration mask for people; Vhey uses her separately reviewed portrait mask.

The prepared master retains original RGB and can contain source colours such as pink. It is not a brand-compliant standalone delivery file. The deterministic renderer remaps illustrations to controlled tints of the exact locked Burst palette. New campaign text, CTA, bars and logo keep exact approved tokens. Final artwork must still pass full-resolution/feed review.

Generative boundary cleanup was tried using the built-in editing tool: retain objects and exact wording, remove detached pixels/fringes, retain transparency and use brand hues. A second pass requested removal of outside glow and all silhouette residue. Those trials did not consistently meet the edge requirement and are not registered. They do not replace the accepted pixel-preserving files.

## Acceptance

Compare embedded text with the source, confirm original RGB hashes, and inspect complete shapes and holes on navy and light backgrounds at full resolution and reduced size. Reject detached pixels, damaged shapes, unreadable copy or unwanted palette changes. Registered crop bounds frame visible content with a small clear margin. Inspect every campaign again; an asset review does not approve arbitrary copy, composition or offers.
