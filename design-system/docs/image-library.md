## Production rules

Select one coherent panel or scene for the audience and message, record its filename and crop coordinates, and preserve the people and environment without generative alteration. Do not imply that depicted people are Burst SMS customers, employees, partners or testimonial participants. Do not infer identities, employers, endorsements, results or protected traits.

Every crop, retouch, colour treatment or branded composition is new creative. Assemble the exact approved Burst SMS Philippines lockup, typography, colours, CTA and copy deterministically, then run the complete brand, content, evidence, accessibility and local-fit checks before display or delivery. Library approval never bypasses final-artwork validation.
