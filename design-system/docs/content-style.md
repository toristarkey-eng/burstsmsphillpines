# Burst SMS Philippines content style

## Position

Burst SMS Philippines helps organisations communicate quickly, reliably, and at scale. Content should make business messaging easier to understand and act on.

## Voice principles

### Useful before promotional

Answer the reader's question before asking for action. Explain what a feature changes for their work.

### Confident and specific

Use concrete language. State the sender, audience, action, and outcome. Replace broad claims with evidence or a plain description.

### Human with restraint

Write warmly and directly. A small playful touch is welcome when the topic allows it, but clarity comes first.

### Local without stereotypes

Use Philippines-specific context when it helps: local industries, business hours, currency, compliance, customer behaviours, and practical examples. Do not use a flag, slang, or a cultural reference as a substitute for relevance.

## Mechanics

- Use British English.
- Use `you` for the reader and `we` for the brand.
- Use active voice and the Oxford comma.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Explain abbreviations on first use.
- Use descriptive link text.
- Use sentence case for headings and controls.
- Use the official market name `Burst SMS Philippines`.

## Claims

Do not publish numbers, rankings, delivery guarantees, pricing, product availability, certifications, or compliance statements without a current approved source. Put the evidence beside the claim during review.

## Calls to action

Name the destination: `Talk to our team`, `Explore SMS solutions`, `Review compliance guidance`, or `Start a trial`. Use one primary action per section.
