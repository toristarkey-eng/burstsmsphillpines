# Logo use

The Burst SMS logo remains the primary identifying asset. Use only the supplied artwork in `assets/brand/`.

## Approved files

- `burst-sms-logo-primary.png` for white and light surfaces.
- `burst-sms-logo-dark.png` for dark surfaces.
- `burst-sms-logo-tagline.png` only where the legacy tagline is still approved.

## Philippines lockup

Set `PHILIPPINES` as a separate Noto Sans semibold label to the right of the primary logo. Use navy or violet, 0.12em letter spacing, and a visual gap of about half the logo height. Do not edit the logo file to add the market name.

## Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the cap height of `SMS` around the logo. For digital use, keep the main lockup at least 120px wide. Use a simpler mark only when approved artwork for that mark exists.

## Do not

Do not stretch, redraw, recolour, outline, shadow, rotate, crop, or place the logo on a busy image. Do not reconstruct the speech bubble or wordmark with live type.
