# Accessibility

## Colour

Use navy or ink for body copy on white, cloud, blush, and cyan. Use white on navy and violet. Do not set small white text on cyan or mint. Pair status colours with text or an icon.

## Typography

Use at least 16px for body text on the web. Keep line length near 45–75 characters. Avoid all caps beyond short labels.

## Interaction

Every interactive control needs a visible keyboard focus state. Use descriptive control labels and maintain a logical tab order. Touch targets should be at least 44px high.

## Media

Write meaningful alt text for informative imagery. Mark decorative imagery as decorative. Caption video and provide a transcript when the message depends on speech.

## Motion

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid rapid flashing and do not make motion the only way to understand a change in state.
